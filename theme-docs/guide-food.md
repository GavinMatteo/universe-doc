# Build for success: Food and drink

A step-by-step guide to building a high-converting home page for a food or drink brand using Universe.

Food and drink stores succeed when they make the product look irresistible, communicate quality and origin clearly, and make purchasing feel simple. This guide walks you through the recommended section order and configuration.

---

## Recommended home page structure

### 1. Bold Slideshow or Promo Banner — hero

For food brands, the hero should make the product look delicious. Use a close-up product shot as the main image rather than a wide lifestyle shot.

If you have a single hero product or offer (for example, a seasonal bundle or subscription box), use the **Promo Banner** instead of the slideshow. The Promo Banner's large data value display (for example, "70%" or "3x") is effective for communicating a key offer immediately.

### 2. Scrolling Text — trust signals

Use Scrolling Text to communicate key trust signals: "No artificial ingredients, Cold pressed, Free delivery over €50". Keep each item to 3 to 5 words.

### 3. Featured Collection — product catalog

Show your main product range in a 3 or 4 column grid. For food stores, use a square or portrait image ratio and consistent photography style (all white backgrounds, or all lifestyle).

Enable **Show vendor** if you carry multiple brands.

### 4. Featured Data — brand credibility

Use the Featured Data section to communicate key numbers: years in business, number of customers, countries shipped to, or certifications. Each counter animates from zero when it scrolls into view.

### 5. Image With Text — origin and process story

Food buyers want to know where the product comes from and how it is made. Use one or two Image With Text sections to tell this story. One image of the source (farm, grove, vineyard), one image of the production process.

Keep text concise. 3 to 4 sentences per section maximum.

### 6. Product Offer Comparison — tiered options

If you have tiered products (for example, a standard, premium, and subscription option), use Product Offer Comparison to display them side by side with feature bullet points. Highlight the option you want to sell most.

### 7. Reviews Carousel — social proof

For food brands, reviews that mention taste, quality, and repeat purchase are the most persuasive. Prioritize reviews with specific product mentions.

---

## 💡 Tips for food and drink stores

- The Ingredients Table block on the Product Page is especially useful for food products. Add it to display nutritional or ingredient information clearly.
- Use the FAQ section on product pages to answer common questions about ingredients, allergens, storage, and delivery.
- The Announcement Bar is valuable for time-limited offers (flash sales, seasonal availability). Update it regularly.
