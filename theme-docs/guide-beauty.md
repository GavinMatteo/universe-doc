# Build for success: Health and beauty

A step-by-step guide to building a high-converting home page for a health or beauty brand using Universe.

Health and beauty stores succeed when they communicate efficacy, build trust through social proof, and guide visitors toward their best-fit product. This guide walks you through the recommended section order and configuration.

---

## Recommended home page structure

### 1. Product Hero Slideshow — editorial hero

The Product Hero Slideshow's parallax effect gives a premium, editorial feel that works well for beauty brands. Use a high-quality product or lifestyle image with a short, benefit-focused heading ("Skin that glows from within").

Set the background text to a relevant keyword (for example, your brand name or a key ingredient) at low opacity.

### 2. Icon List — key benefits

Immediately below the hero, add an Icon List section with 3 to 4 cards communicating your core product benefits. For example: "Dermatologist tested", "Clean ingredients", "Cruelty free", "Results in 28 days".

Keep each card to one heading and one short sentence. Use the icon library to find relevant icons.

### 3. Featured Collection — product range

Show your product range in a 3-column grid. For beauty products, a portrait image ratio (showing the product in hand or in context) typically converts better than a square.

Enable **Show color swatches** for products with color variants (for example, foundation shades or lip colors).

### 4. Spotlighted Product — hero product

Feature your best-selling or most important product with the Spotlighted Product section. Add a short scrolling text with the product's key ingredient or key benefit.

Use Feature Slide blocks to add additional product angle images alongside the hero shot.

### 5. Before and After Slider — visible results

If your product produces a visible result, the Before and After Slider section is one of the highest-impact elements you can add. Show a before-and-after comparison with the slider. Label each side clearly.

### 6. Reviews Carousel — clinical social proof

For health and beauty, reviews that mention specific results ("After 4 weeks my skin is clearer"), skin types, or ages are the most persuasive. Select reviews that reflect your target customer.

Include reviewer photos. Cards with photos significantly outperform text-only cards in this category.

### 7. Featured Data — results and credibility

Use the Featured Data section to show key metrics: percentage of users who saw results, number of reviews, years of research, or number of active ingredients. These numbers reinforce efficacy claims.

### 8. FAQ section — objection handling

Add a FAQ section at the bottom of the home page addressing the most common purchase objections: "Is it suitable for sensitive skin?", "How long until I see results?", "Can I use it with other products?".

---

## 💡 Tips for health and beauty stores

- The Comparison Table section is excellent for beauty brands with a product line (for example, comparing a basic, advanced, and professional version of a serum).
- Use the Sticky Section to walk visitors through your 3-step routine or your key ingredient story, with each Content block representing one step.
- For products with ingredients tables or clinical studies, use the Accordion block on the Product Page to keep the page clean while making information accessible.
